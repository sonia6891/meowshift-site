What is Meowshift?

Meowshift is made for people who work shifts: nurses, caregivers, factory, retail, security and hospitality staff, and part-timers. Add your shifts to see which days you work, when you finish and roughly what you'll earn this month.

What it does

SHIFT CALENDAR

  • Day, evening, night and off shifts on one calendar; use 2 on 2 off, 2-2-3 or the 5-day week as they are, or set up your company's own rotation
  • Different hours each day: change any single day's times, and log half-day and hourly leave too
  • Select several days and fill them in one go, with undo

PAY

  • Monthly, hourly and part-time pay, with hours added up from your schedule
  • Several jobs, each with its own pay and payday, on one payday calendar
  • Overtime at your company's own rates

LEAVE

  • Your company's annual, sick and personal leave; balances update as you book time off, with a reminder before leave expires

EVERY DAY

  • Shift alarms for day, evening and night shifts, skipped on days off
  • Your own Meow Assistant: give it a name, then talk or type one sentence to plan shifts, book leave, log overtime or change your hours; it also chats with you about work and how you feel
  • Swap ledger: which day you swapped and who owes whom a shift

Free and PRO

🎁 Full PRO for your first 7 days: every PRO feature is yours for the first 7 days after you download, with no payment and no automatic charge. After that, keep the basics for free or subscribe to Meowshift PRO.

FREE

  • 1 job, this month's and next month's schedule, shift alarms, this month's estimated total and a payday reminder the day before, plus overtime mood cards
  • Your data stays and carries over when you upgrade to PRO

MEOWSHIFT PRO (SUBSCRIPTION)

  • Leave balances and expiry reminders
  • Widgets, Apple Watch, and a live countdown in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock ScreeniOS
